About JimmyWithFiona

JimmyWithFiona is a collection of free online tools, games, and fun personality tests. All apps run entirely in your browser — no downloads, no signups, no data collection. Whether you need to count words for an essay, play 2048 on your lunch break, test your love compatibility, or check your BMI, we've got you covered.

What's Inside?

Tools: Mortgage calculator, unit converter, QR code generator, password generator, color tool, countdown timer, random number generator, stopwatch, word counter, age calculator, percentage calculator, and BMI calculator. These tools cover everyday needs from financial planning to health monitoring to productivity. All calculations and conversions happen instantly in your browser.

Games: Wordle daily word puzzle, 2048 puzzle game, brick breaker arcade, and a Windows 98 simulator with working Notepad, Calculator, Paint, and Minesweeper. Each game features smooth animations, touch controls, and local saving. The Windows 98 simulator lets you drag windows, use the Start menu, and relive the classic desktop experience.

Skill & Tests: Typing speed test (WPM and accuracy), personality quizzes, love calculator, psychological age test, and a spinning wheel for random decisions. Each test generates shareable results — perfect for sharing with friends on social media.

News: WorldPulse live international news dashboard with real-time headlines from top global outlets and world clocks for major cities.

Why Use JimmyWithFiona?

Everything is 100% free with no limits. All tools work offline once loaded, respect your privacy by processing data locally, and are optimized for both desktop and mobile devices. Bookmark this page for quick access to all your everyday tools and games.

Privacy & Security

Your privacy is our priority. All tools process data locally in your browser — your text, calculations, passwords, and drawings never leave your device. We don't require accounts, don't track your inputs, and don't store your data. Read our full privacy policy for details.
