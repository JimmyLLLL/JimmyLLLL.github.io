Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

JimmyWithFiona ("we", "this site") is a collection of browser-based tools, games, and tests. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require registration and do not collect personal information such as your name, email, or phone number. All tool operations — text counting, calculations, QR generation, password creation, drawings, game scores — happen entirely in your browser. Your data is never transmitted to or stored on any server.

2. Local Storage

Some tools use your browser's local storage to save preferences, such as high scores in games or visit counters. This data remains on your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings. It is never sent to us.

3. Cookies

This site itself does not set cookies. However, third-party services described below may use cookies.

4. Third-Party Services

This site uses the following third-party services:

  • GitHub Pages — Hosts this website. GitHub may log standard request data (IP address, access time) for security purposes.
  • Google AdSense — Displays advertisements. Google uses cookies to serve relevant ads.
  • Counter API — Provides anonymous page view counting. No personal data is transmitted.
  • Hacker News API — Used by WorldPulse for live news headlines. No personal data is sent.

5. Advertising

This site displays ads via Google AdSense (publisher ID: ca-pub-1840337371416691). Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You may opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info.
  • For more information, see Google's advertising privacy policy.

6. Third-Party Links

Our site contains links to external websites (news outlets, search engines, etc.). We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Our tools and games are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other users. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete. Your tool data is stored locally and can be cleared via your browser settings.

9. Data Security

All data is processed in your browser using standard web technologies. Our site is served over HTTPS to ensure secure transmission. We do not maintain servers or databases that store user data.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. We recommend reviewing it periodically.

11. Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy, please visit our contact page.
