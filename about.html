About JimmyWithFiona

Free online tools, games, and fun tests — all in one place.

Our Mission

JimmyWithFiona is a collection of free, fast, and privacy-friendly web applications. We believe that useful online tools should be accessible to everyone — no downloads, no signups, no hidden costs. Every tool on our site runs entirely in your browser, meaning your data never leaves your device.

What We Offer

Our platform hosts 10 distinct web applications across four categories:

  • Tools: Mortgage calculator, unit converter, QR code generator, password generator, color tool, countdown timer, random number generator, stopwatch, word counter, and BMI calculator. These cover everyday needs from finance to health to productivity.
  • Games: 2048 puzzle game, classic brick breaker arcade, and a fully interactive Windows 98 simulator with working Notepad, Calculator, Paint, and Minesweeper.
  • Fun Tests: Personality quizzes including "What Animal Are You?", a love compatibility calculator, psychological age test, and a spinning wheel for random decisions. Perfect for sharing with friends.
  • News: WorldPulse, a live international news dashboard with real-time headlines from top global outlets and world clocks.

Privacy First

All tools process data locally in your browser. We do not require accounts, do not collect personal information, and do not store your inputs. Your text, calculations, drawings, and game scores exist only in your browser session. Read our full privacy policy for details.

How We Stay Free

JimmyWithFiona is supported by advertising through Google AdSense. Ads help us cover hosting costs and keep all tools free for everyone. We never charge for access to any tool, game, or test on our site.

Technology

All applications are built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no heavy dependencies. This ensures fast loading times, broad compatibility, and minimal resource usage. The entire site is hosted on GitHub Pages with a custom domain and HTTPS.

Have feedback, found a bug, or want to suggest a new tool? Visit our contact page — we'd love to hear from you!
