Free Browser Games — No Download Needed

Play 6 classic games instantly in your browser. No downloads, no installations, no signups — just click and play. Our games run on HTML5 and JavaScript, so they work on any modern browser including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. All games are fully responsive and support both keyboard/mouse and touch controls for mobile play.

Wordle — Daily Word Puzzle

Wordle is the viral word-guessing game that took the world by storm. Guess a hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries. After each guess, tiles change color: green = correct letter in the right spot, yellow = letter in the word but wrong spot, gray = letter not in the word. Our version includes a daily puzzle plus unlimited practice mode, stats tracking, and emoji sharing.

Minesweeper — Classic Puzzle Game

The timeless minesweeper game that came with Windows. Left-click to reveal squares, right-click to flag mines. Use logic and deduction to clear the board without hitting a mine. Three difficulty levels: Beginner (9x9, 10 mines), Intermediate (16x16, 40 mines), and Expert (30x16, 99 mines).

2048 — Addictive Sliding Puzzle

Swipe to move numbered tiles. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one. The goal is to create a tile showing 2048. Simple to learn, hard to master. Keep your highest tile in a corner and build chains along the edges for the best strategy.

Snake Game — Retro Arcade Classic

The classic snake game from old Nokia phones. Eat food to grow longer, avoid hitting walls or your own tail. Three difficulty levels affect the snake's speed. How long can you survive?
