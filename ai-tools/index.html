AI Tools — Free & Private

Our AI tools collection brings you 5 powerful artificial intelligence utilities that run 100% in your browser. Unlike cloud-based AI tools that require you to upload your photos to a server, our tools process everything locally using JavaScript and Canvas API. Your images never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy and security.

AI Face Swap Online

Our AI Face Swap tool uses face-api.js with TinyFaceDetector and 68-point facial landmarks to detect faces, then applies Delaunay triangulation and Lab color space blending for realistic face swaps. All processing happens in your browser — no server, no upload, complete privacy.

AI Avatar Generator

Transform your photos into 6 different art styles: cartoon, anime, oil painting, sketch, cyberpunk, and vintage. The avatar generator applies pixel-level Canvas filters including bilateral smoothing, color quantization, edge detection, and Lab color space adjustments.

AI Background Remover

Remove image backgrounds instantly with AI. The tool uses on-device machine learning to detect foreground subjects and produce transparent PNG output. Perfect for product photos, profile pictures, and graphic design — no upload required.

Meme Generator with Text & Stickers

Create memes from your photos with top/bottom text, stickers, filters, and 10+ classic meme templates. Choose from Impact, Comic Sans, or Bold fonts, 7 text colors, 12 stickers, and 6 image filters. Download as PNG or copy to clipboard.
